1. Ultimate guide to profiling Unity games, by Unity team.
https://unity.com/resources/ultimate-guide-to-profiling-unity-games

This is the most essential and must-read book about Unity profiling and optimization. This book will teach you about processes of profiling - how to create and integrate them into your development process. It reveals questions how, what and when to measure and optimize. It will save you a lot of time - this is only 75 page book so you can read it in one-sitting instead of wasting time on discovering what are the most appropriate and effective processes of optimizing by yourself. If you are still using FPS as the main metric of your perfomance, wasting time on making profiling builds by yourself on your local machine, trying to find perfomance drops using your perception, and optimizing random stuff based on assumptions hoping for improvement then you must read this book!
2. Optimize your console and PC game performance, by Unity team.
https://unity.com/resources/optimize-your-console-and-pc-game-performance

This book will teach you about main techniques and tips to optimize your project, such as culling, draw call batching, LODs, lightning optimization and baking, mipmaps, overdraw reduction and more. It covers how to optimize your rendering, particles, UI, assets, physics, animations, project configurations in general.
3. Optimize Your Mobile Game Performance, by Unity team.
https://unity.com/resources/unity-e-book-optimize-your-mobile-game-performance

This book has a lot in common with the previous one, but it goes deeper and deeper into optimization techniques, as on mobile devices, optimization is especially important in resource-constrained environments. For example, for more powerful devices, you can have over 300 draw calls at the time, GPU-heavy effects and particles, large textures, etc., but on mobile devices, this is killer. This book goes into more detail about how you can get the most out of these devices, as well as the sacrifices and compromises you need to make in order to create a comfortable gaming experience.
4. TheGameDevGuru resource, by Ruben.
https://thegamedev.guru/blog

Ruben is known in the Unity Community as a very knowledgeable person in the field of optimization. On his website you can find even more optimization techniques, various checklists of an optimized project, tables and step-by-step optimization schemes, performance cost tables for standard unity shaders and a lot of other useful information. Here you can find a lot of free information, but there is also a subscription if you want to become the best in optimization and take your project to the next level!

SkinnedMeshRenderer (for example characters), we can’t use instancing, because the skinning is calculated on the CPU, and submitted to the GPU one by one.
